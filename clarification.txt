- What tags did you filter by?

**Action:** Open your scraping scripts (e.g., `scripts/scrape_stackoverflow.py`, `scripts/scrape_github.py`) and document what they do.

#### 3. Pre Gen AI / Post Gen AI
**Source:** The `creation_date` or `timestamp` field in your existing posts.
**Action:** Filter your existing posts by date:

**No re-scraping needed** — just filter what you have.

#### 4. Post Get
**Source:** The total number of raw results you got from your API calls.
**Action:** Count the total posts in each raw JSON file.
```python

    posts = json.load(f)

```

#### 5. Inclusion Criteria / Exclusion Criteria
**Source:** Your existing filtering logic in your scraping scripts.
**Action:** Document what filters you already applied.

If your scraper has:
```python
if post["score"] >= 0 and len(post["body"]) >= 100:
    # Include this post
```
Then your Inclusion Criteria is: `"Score >= 0; Body >= 100 chars"`

If your scraper has:
```python
if post["is_closed"] or post["is_duplicate"]:
    continue  # Skip this post
```
Then your Exclusion Criteria is: `"Closed posts; Duplicates"`

#### 6. Total Post
**Source:** The count after applying inclusion/exclusion.
**Action:** Count the posts that passed your filters.
```python
# If you stored filtered results
total_post = len(filtered_posts)

# Or calculate from raw
total_post = post_get - excluded_count
```

#### 7. Time Period
**Source:** The earliest and latest dates in your dataset.
**Action:** Find min and max dates.
```python
dates = [p["creation_date"] for p in posts]
time_period = f"{min(dates)} to {max(dates)}"
```

## Do You Need to Re-Scrape? Decision Framework

### ❌ NO Re-Scraping Needed If:

| Situation | Why No Re-Scrape |
|-----------|------------------|
| You already have posts from all 11 forums | Just profile what you have |
| Your posts have `creation_date` fields | Just filter by date |
| Your scraping scripts used correct keywords/tags | Just document them |
| Your posts have all required metadata (title, body, tags, score) | Just count them |

### ⚠️ MAYBE Re-Scrape If:

| Situation | What To Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| A forum's JSON file is empty or missing | Re-run that specific scraper |
| Some posts are missing `creation_date` | Check if the API provides it; if not, note "Unknown" in Time Period |
| Your original search used wrong keywords | Re-scrape with corrected keywords |
| You need more posts from a forum that has very few | Re-scrape with broader search terms |

### ✅ YES Re-Scrape If:

| Situation | What To Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| You discover your original scrape was incomplete (e.g., only got page 1 of results) | Re-scrape with pagination |
| Your professor requires a specific tag that wasn't in your original search | Re-scrape with the new tag |
| Your time period doesn't cover the required range | Re-scrape with date filters |
